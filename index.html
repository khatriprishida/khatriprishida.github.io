Financial Statement Analysis and Company Valuation

Illustrative model — not investment advice

Simplified Gordon Growth model built to demonstrate valuation mechanics. FCF₀ of $5.90/share is a normalized illustrative figure, not an Amazon financial disclosure. Prior price of $239.89 is the reference price used in the original coursework.

Single-stage Gordon Growth: P₀ = FCF₀ × (1 + g) ÷ (r − g). Note the sensitivity: at a 2.2pp spread, ±50bps on either input moves value by roughly a third.
