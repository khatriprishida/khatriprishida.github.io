01 — Profile

A generalist analyst, comfortable from the ledger to the dashboard.

The work runs in one line: get the data clean, build the model, test what it is sensitive to, then present it so that a non-analyst can act on it.

Financial Analyst with an MBA in Financial Analysis (GPA 3.90) and a strong foundation in FP&A, budgeting, forecasting, financial modeling, valuation, business intelligence, and data analytics.

Proficient in Advanced Excel (VLOOKUP, Power Pivot, Power Query, pivot tables), SQL, Power BI, and R to transform financial and operational data into actionable insights.

Experienced in KPI reporting, dashboard development, financial statement analysis, investment analysis, variance analysis, and cross-functional collaboration.
